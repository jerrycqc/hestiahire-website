Customer support had become the founder's unpaid second shift, and it was the first thing she wanted off her plate once the brand started actually making money.

**A backlog with no fixed owner**

Before onboarding a support assistant, tickets sat in a shared inbox with no assigned owner and no target response time. The founder cleared it when she had energy left at the end of the day, which meant response time depended entirely on how her day had gone, not on any actual standard.

**Building a playbook first**

Rather than handing over the inbox on day one, we spent the first week building a response playbook: standard answers for the most common ten questions, escalation rules for anything policy-sensitive, and a tone guide pulled from the founder's own past replies so customers wouldn't notice a difference.

**What changed**

Once the assistant took over, response time dropped from days to hours, and CSAT scores on resolved tickets climbed noticeably as customers started getting same-day answers instead of waiting. The founder now sees only the handful of tickets each week that actually need her judgment call.
