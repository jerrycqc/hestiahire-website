In real estate, the agent who responds first often wins the lead, and she knew it, which made the response-time problem more than just an inconvenience.

**The real cost of a 3-hour lag**

We looked at three months of her lead data before matching her with anyone. Leads she responded to within 30 minutes converted to a showing at a meaningfully higher rate than ones she reached after a few hours, and a noticeable share of the slow-response leads had already gone with another agent by the time she called back.

**Splitting the calendar from the relationship**

The assistant took over calendar management and first-touch response entirely, using a simple qualifying script to sort serious buyers from early browsers. Serious leads get a same-hour call attempt and showing options; everyone else gets a warm, lighter-touch follow-up sequence that doesn't demand the broker's immediate time.

**Results**

Average lead response time dropped from roughly 3 hours to under 20 minutes. The broker's own tracking shows a clear uptick in showing-to-offer conversion over the following two quarters, which she attributes directly to leads no longer going cold before she can reach them.
