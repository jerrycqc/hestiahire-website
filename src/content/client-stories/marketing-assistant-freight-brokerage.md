Freight brokerage is a relationship business, and both owners had built theirs entirely through the phone. The problem was that phone-only growth has a hard ceiling: you can only call so many shippers in a week.

**Marketing had never existed as a function**

Before HestiaHire, there was no content calendar, no LinkedIn presence to speak of, and a website that hadn't been updated since the company launched. Every single new shipper account came from an outbound call, which meant growth was capped by how many calls two owners could physically make.

**Building inbound around what they already know**

The marketing assistant didn't invent expertise, she packaged it. Monthly capacity and rate updates came directly from what the owners already track internally for their own operations; LinkedIn outreach used the same lane specialties (temperature-controlled, flatbed, specific regional corridors) the sales team already leads with on calls.

**Results**

Inbound inquiries climbed from about one a month to nine within the first quarter, and four of those closed as new accounts, sourced entirely from marketing rather than outbound calling. The owners now spend their calling time on warmer leads instead of cold ones.
