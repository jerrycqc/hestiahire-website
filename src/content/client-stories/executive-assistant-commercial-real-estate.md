In commercial leasing, the broker who responds first to a tenant rep often gets the tour scheduled first, and a scheduled tour is most of the way to a deal. He knew it, which made the response-time problem more than an inconvenience.

**The real cost of a 4-hour lag**

We looked at three months of his lead data before matching him with anyone. Inquiries he responded to within 30 minutes converted to a scheduled tour at a meaningfully higher rate than ones he reached after several hours, and a noticeable share of the slow-response inquiries had already toured a competing space by the time he called back.

**Splitting the calendar from the relationship**

The assistant took over calendar management and first-touch response entirely, using a simple qualifying script to sort serious tenant reps from early-stage inquiries. Serious leads get a same-hour call attempt and tour options; everyone else gets a lighter-touch follow-up sequence that doesn't demand the broker's immediate time.

**Results**

Average lead response time dropped from roughly 4 hours to under 25 minutes. The broker's own tracking shows a clear uptick in tour-to-LOI conversion over the following two quarters, which he attributes directly to inquiries no longer going cold before he can reach them.
