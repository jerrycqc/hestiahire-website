For a wholesale distributor, a slow answer to a business account isn't just a bad experience, it's a reason for that account to start sourcing from a competitor instead.

**A backlog with no fixed owner**

Before onboarding a support assistant, business-account tickets sat in a shared inbox with no assigned owner and no target response time. The operations manager cleared it whenever other duties allowed, which meant response time depended entirely on how busy operations was that week, not on any actual service standard.

**Building a playbook around real account rules**

Rather than handing over the inbox on day one, we spent the first two weeks building a response playbook: order and backorder status lookups, the distributor's tiered account pricing rules, and escalation triggers for anything touching credit or custom pricing, so the assistant could answer most business-account questions without looping in sales.

**What changed**

Once the assistant took over, response time dropped from days to hours. The account team noticed something else too: buyers who had previously cited slow support as a reason they were evaluating other distributors stopped raising it, and renewal conversations got noticeably easier.
