Six studio locations should have meant six times the marketing output. Instead, the owner found herself writing captions at 11pm the night before a launch, and then nothing again for three weeks.

**The real problem wasn't ideas, it was consistency**

She had plenty of things worth posting: new instructor hires, member transformation stories, seasonal class formats. What she didn't have was a reliable weekly rhythm to get any of it published. Content lived in her head and her phone's camera roll, not in a calendar anyone could execute against.

**Building the weekly rhythm**

Her HestiaHire marketing assistant set up a simple recurring structure: Monday planning, Tuesday email, three social posts a week per location, and a monthly content batch call with the owner to gather raw material (photos, instructor updates, upcoming promotions). The assistant handles the writing, scheduling, and basic graphic templates; the owner just approves.

**Where it landed**

Within a quarter, email open rates nearly doubled and social-driven class sign-ups were up 40%. More importantly to the owner, marketing stopped being the thing she scrambled to do at the last minute, since it now runs on a schedule regardless of how busy the studios get.
